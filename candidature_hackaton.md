```markdown
### 💡 Inspiration & Problem Statement
Building production-ready Machine Learning from raw enterprise tabular data is fundamentally broken: silent data leaks, hallucinated pipelines, massive token bills, and zero regulatory compliance. Enterprise data scientists waste 80% of their time fixing repetitive data quality issues and struggling to provide transparent model explanations to auditors.

We built **DATASET AUTOMATOR** to transform raw enterprise tabular datasets into fully audited, production-ready ML pipelines with mathematical certificates in 60 seconds — completely eliminating tedious manual scripting while guaranteeing uncompromised trust and governance.

---

### 🚀 Key Features & Multi-Agent Architecture

1. **Spatial Execution Canvas (60 FPS Graph Engineering)**:
   * Replaces overwhelming chat interfaces with an interactive, GPU-accelerated spatial canvas.
   * **Merged Step Cards** unify agent roles, models, and tools into single visual blocks with live SVG Bézier curve animations.
   * **Faded Pruned Ghost Nodes** visualize rejected or speculative branches to give operators full context awareness.

2. **Google Foundation Model Champion (Google TabFM)**:
   * Leverages pre-trained tabular foundation models outperforming traditional XGBoost/LightGBM algorithms without overfitting.

3. **Google PAIR What-If Tool (WIT)**:
   * Real-time sensitivity probing, demographic parity audit, and **Nearest Counterfactual Search** to compute actionable steps for reversing sensitive AI decisions.

4. **Google Model Card Toolkit (MCT)**:
   * Automated generation of Google-standardized interactive model identity cards in Material Design HTML and JSON formats.

5. **Autonomous Red Teamer Sub-Agent**:
   * Pre-delivery automated stress-testing across 4 adversarial vectors: Target Leakage, Outlier Spikes (+500%), Noise Injection, and Ethical Bias.

6. **Adaptive Model Router (125× Cost Reduction)**:
   * Cascaded routing between Google TabFM (22ms), local Small Language Models (152ms), and Gemini 3.5 Flash — slashing token expenditures by a factor of 125×.

7. **EU AI Act Cryptographic Attestation (Articles 12 & 26 / NIST AI RMF)**:
   * Immutable digital black box signing every pipeline execution trace with **RSASSA-PSS-SHA256** and SKOPS safe serialization.

8. **Antigravity Copilot (Conversational MLOps Assistant)**:
   * Natural language copilot equipped with a Google Cloud / Vertex AI multi-model selector (Gemini 3.5 Flash, Gemini 3.5 Pro, Gemma 2, Google TabFM) and real-time telemetry.

9. **CRISP-ML Forensic Notebook Validator**:
   * Generates clean, reproducible 55-cell Jupyter notebooks structured across 14 standard CRISP-ML(Q) sections, verified with a perfect **100/100 EXCELLENT** audit score.

---

### 🛠️ Built With & Technologies Used

* **Google AI Models**: Google Gemini 3.5 Flash, Google Gemini 3.5 Pro, Google TabFM, Gemma 2 27B IT
* **Google Frameworks**: Google PAIR What-If Tool (WIT), Google Model Card Toolkit (MCT), Google AI Studio
* **Data & Ingestion**: Google BigQuery Zero-ETL (`bigframes`), DuckDB In-Memory OLAP Engine (<10ms), Pandas, NumPy
* **ML & Governance**: Scikit-Learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost, SHAP, LIME, Optuna, SKOPS, Cryptography (RSASSA-PSS)
* **Knowledge & Memory**: Neo4j GraphRAG (117 OKF ontology cards, 407 relations), Multi-Tier SQLite with TTL decay, LargeJson offloader
* **Frontend & Visualization**: Streamlit (Dark-First Mineral Black `#09090b` palette), GPU SVG Native `<animateMotion>`, vis.js

---

### 🧠 Key Learnings & Insights

1. **Foundation Models for Tabular Data**: Specialized tabular foundation models like Google TabFM provide unprecedented robustness against noisy features and extreme outliers compared to traditional boosted trees.
2. **Cost-Arbitrage with Cascading SLMs**: Offloading intermediate telemetry verification to lightweight models cuts total LLM operating costs by 125× without sacrificing accuracy.
3. **Cryptographic Proofs as a Business Catalyst**: Transforming compliance constraints (EU AI Act) into automated cryptographic proofs (`RSASSA-PSS-SHA256`) turns regulatory overhead into a decisive enterprise trust asset.

---

### 🔭 What's Next for Dataset Automator
* One-click containerized deployment to Google Cloud Run and Vertex AI Endpoints.
* Automated OpenTelemetry (OTLP) live streaming to Google Cloud Monitoring.
* Multimodal pipeline summary cards and explanation reels generated with Google Veo and Imagen.
```
